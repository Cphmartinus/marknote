## What's new in 1.2.2

- **Right-click menu in the style of Word.** Right-click in the Markdown pane or in the formatted document to get:
  - a formatting bar with heading style, bold, italic, strikethrough, highlight, code, bulleted, numbered and checklists, and *New comment*
  - Cut, Copy and *Paste Options* (paste, or keep text only)
  - Link…, an *Insert* submenu (table, picture, diagram, formula, footnote, horizontal line), New comment and Select all
  - *Open link* and *Copy link address* on links, and *Edit diagram…* on diagrams

  The menu follows the light, dark and sepia themes and is available in all 13 languages.

### Downloads

- `MarkNote-Setup-1.2.2.exe`: installer. **Recommended.** Install it over your current version; your settings are kept.
- `MarkNote-1.2.2-portable.exe`: runs without installation.
- `MarkNote-user-guide.docx` and `MarkNote-vejledning.docx`: user guides in English and Danish.

The files are not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may show a warning the first time. Choose **More info → Run anyway**.

If you have MarkNote 1.1.0 or later installed, you can also get this update with *Help → Check for updates*.
